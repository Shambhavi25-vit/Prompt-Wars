# ScholarFlow - AI Student Workspace

Live Application: https://scholarflow-workspace.netlify.app

An intelligent, all-in-one student workspace designed to transform course documents into deep academic summaries, high-yield revision notes, interactive quizzes, and automated deadline tracking.

---

## Features

### 1. Comprehensive Document Summarization
- Upload lecture slides, PDFs, notes, or scanned documents.
- Automatically synthesizes an exhaustive, topic-by-topic academic breakdown of the entire document.
- Fully explains all mechanisms, theorems, mathematical derivations, step-by-step procedures, and worked examples without superficial cutting or omission.

### 2. High-Yield Revision Notes & Exam Strategies
- Concurrently generates dedicated exam preparation material in a separate panel.
- Includes quick-recall bullet points for every topic, pro-tips, and mental shortcuts to solve complex problems rapidly.
- Features a structured table of core formulas, rules, and algorithms alongside common exam traps and pitfalls to avoid mark deductions.
- Provides a 5-minute memory cheat sheet for rapid pre-exam review.

### 3. Interactive Exam Quiz Generator
- Directly after reviewing the summary and notes, test your knowledge with an interactive quiz powered by your uploaded materials.
- Dynamically scales question count based on document depth (from 5-6 questions for quick notes up to 20 questions for comprehensive lecture packs).
- Features realistic multiple-choice questions, instant answer validation, detailed academic explanations, and performance scorecards.

### 4. Natural Language Deadline & Action Item Parser
- Type assignments and deadlines in natural language (English or Hinglish), such as:
  - *"I have to complete my maths assignment by tomorrow"*
  - *"complete chemistry lab report before 15th Sept"*
  - *"mujhe kal apna assignment submit karna hai"*
- Automatically parses the clean task title, calculates the target calendar date, and displays countdown badges and overdue warnings.

### 5. Multi-Document Support & Flexible Inputs
- Upload single or multiple documents at once (PDFs, Word documents, text files, and images).
- Includes built-in OCR and client-side extraction so scanned lecture notes and slides are processed seamlessly.

### 6. Study Session History & Past Conversations
- Automatically saves your study sessions, summaries, notes, and task lists.
- Supports instant offline access using local browser storage alongside optional cloud database synchronization, allowing you to return to past conversations and notes anytime.
